StreamClipper Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

StreamClipper is a private desktop app run by the owner of the YouTube channel @Str3amerzCl1pz. It turns stream highlights into short clips and uploads them to that channel. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, StreamClipper asks for permission to upload videos and manage playlists on that one YouTube channel. It uses this only to post clips, set their titles and descriptions, and add them to playlists.

Where data is kept

The Google sign-in token and all clips stay on the owner's own computer. StreamClipper has no server, does not collect data about viewers or other people, and does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's secrets folder removes the saved token from the computer.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: reply on any video on @Str3amerzCl1pz.
